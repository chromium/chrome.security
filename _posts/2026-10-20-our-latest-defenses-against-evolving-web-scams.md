excerpt: Online scams and social engineering tactics are constantly evolving, but as these threats grow and adapt, so do our defenses. In 2020, we launched the Enhanced Protection mode of Safe Browsing in Chrome to better address these increasingly sophisticated threats, and in 2025, we shared that more than 1 billion Chrome users had it enabled. Since then, we’ve added even more features to Enhanced Protection, making its users ten times safer from phishing, social engineering, notifications abuse, and other scams when compared to Safe Browsing's Standard Protection mode. Here's a look at some of the new features we've introduced in 2026 to further strengthen these protections.
